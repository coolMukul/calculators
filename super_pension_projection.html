Change the super inputs and the pension forecast below updates with them. The pension starting balance is the super nominal balance at retirement.

Important information

This calculator is an educational tool. It gives an illustration of how a super balance could grow and then be drawn down under the assumptions shown, based on the figures you enter. It is not a forecast, and your actual result will differ.

It is not intended to be relied on for making a decision about a financial product. It does not take your personal circumstances into account. Consider getting advice from a financial adviser (an Australian financial services licensee) before making any financial decisions.

Balances are shown in future dollars and also in today's dollars, which allow for inflation. Results rely on the assumptions and limitations listed under Assumptions & limitations and in the notes at the foot of each section.

Assumptions & limitations
* Illustrative only, not financial advice. Monthly compounding; contributions are added at the end of each month. Employer (Super Guarantee) and salary sacrifice contributions are concessional: taxed at 15% on entry, or 30% on the part above the Division 293 income threshold. Amounts over the contribution caps are excluded. Caps and thresholds are held constant (not indexed). The low income super tax offset and government co-contribution use gross salary as the income test, with 2026-27 thresholds held constant. Illustrative income is the real retirement balance drawn down at a fixed annual rate. Net return = gross growth rate minus annual deductions (fees and earnings tax), applied as a nominal annual rate. Real value is discounted by annual inflation. Amounts are in Australian dollars (AUD).
* Age Pension is estimated as the lower of the assets test and the income test (income is deemed from financial assets). Rates and thresholds are as at 20 September 2026 and are set from the Single/Couple and Homeowner/Non-Homeowner selections. Work income: the first $300 a fortnight is exempt (the Work Bonus saved-up balance is not modelled). Estimates only, not advice.
